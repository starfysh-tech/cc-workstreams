Workstreams gauges

Each block is a terminal render. One character is one cell. Colors are the spec hexes from design.md, Visual language. Every gauge is shown on a dark terminal (#1a1a19) and a light terminal (#fcfcfb).

1. Meter anatomy

Full cells are █ in the fill color. The partial cell is an eighth-block in the fill color over the track. Empty cells are track background. │ in muted ink marks auto-compact at 92%. Labels stay in ink.

Zoom: the partial cell of the 64% meter. 64% of 20 cells is 12.8 cells, so 12 full cells and ▊ (6/8).

2. Pace meters

┃ marks the share of the window already elapsed. On the track it is ink. Inside the fill it is a notch in the terminal background color. Projected use at reset is used % ÷ elapsed share. At or below 100% the fill is accent and the label is on pace. From 100% to 120% it is warning and ahead. Above 120% it is critical and runs out ~<time>.

Elapsed and projected at reset: week 72% elapsed, projected 60%. Week 60% elapsed, projected 110%. Week 70% elapsed, projected 136%. 5-hour window 47% elapsed, projected 13%. Reset times are the remaining share of 168 hours or 5 hours.

3. Row mini-meters

3 cells, 24 levels, colored by context severity.

4. Git diverging bar

Removed extends left in red, added extends right in blue, centered on ┊. Cells per side = min(5, ⌈2·log10(n+1)⌉).

5. Row type glyphs

Columns: glyph, type, ask, mini-meter, age, PR. Rows that need you are bright. WORKING and WRAP? recede to muted ink. Age turns warning after 15 minutes of waiting on you.

6. Full panel

The panel docked on the right third, 52 columns wide. List, then a preview of the selected PICK row, then the footer. The footer holds the weekly and 5-hour pace meters. A version chip joins them only when sessions run different versions.

7. Status row

One colored row under the prompt box, drawn by the mod at the hint-line site. Left: model chip, a 10-cell context meter with its auto-compact tick, branch in secondary ink, and the git diverging bar. Right: the engine's existing hint and mode text in muted ink. The first example is 32% accent with −0 +0. The second is 86% critical with −3 +12.

Replaces ccstatusline. Drawn by the mod at the PromptHint site; fallback is a statusLine script emitting ANSI.
